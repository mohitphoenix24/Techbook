# Nayan Traders Employee Handbook

Welcome to Nayan Traders. This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from us and what we expect from you. If something is unclear, ask People Operations at people@nayantraders.example.

## Working hours and remote work

Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. You are free to arrange the rest of your day around them. After your probation ends, you may work remotely up to three days each week. Please tell your manager which days you plan to work from home. Working from a different city or country for more than 20 days in a year needs written approval from People Operations, because it can affect tax and insurance.

## Leave

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave is credited monthly, so a new joiner receives a pro-rated amount. You can carry over up to 6 unused days into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.

Sick leave is 8 days per year. A doctor's note is needed if you are away for more than 2 consecutive days. Maternity leave is 26 weeks on full pay, and paternity leave is 2 weeks on full pay. If a close family member passes away, you can take 5 days of bereavement leave.

Public holidays follow the Maharashtra government list. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, you do not get a day in lieu.

## Expenses and travel

Business expenses are reimbursed within 10 working days when you submit them in the Expenses portal. A receipt is required for any single item above ₹500. Expenses above ₹10,000 need approval from your manager before you spend the money.

Book flights in economy class. In metro cities the hotel limit is ₹6,000 per night, and ₹4,000 elsewhere. While travelling on business you get a food allowance of ₹800 per day, so you do not need to submit meal receipts. If you use your own car, mileage is paid at ₹9 per kilometre. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Equipment and security

New joiners receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset on their first day. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If a device is lost or stolen, report it to the security team within 2 hours.

Use the company password manager and switch on two-factor authentication for every work account. Never store customer data on a personal device. Lock your screen when you step away; laptops lock themselves after 5 minutes.

## Benefits

The company pays for health insurance with a cover of ₹5 lakh that includes your spouse, children and parents. Each employee has a learning budget of ₹40,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. The company contributes to the Provident Fund as required by law. A monthly wellness allowance of ₹1,500 can be used for a gym, yoga or similar.

## Probation and notice period

Probation lasts 6 months, with a review at 3 months. During probation the notice period is 30 days. After probation it is 60 days. Every employee has a weekly one-to-one with their manager.

## Raising a concern

If you face harassment or see something that breaks our code of conduct, talk to your manager or to People Operations. You can also use the confidential ethics line, which accepts anonymous reports. Nobody will be penalised for raising a concern in good faith.
