# Wireless Earbuds Pro: FAQ and warranty

## Battery and charging

The earbuds play for up to 8 hours on a single charge. The charging case holds three extra charges, which gives up to 24 hours in total. A quick 15-minute charge gives about 3 hours of playback. A full charge of the case takes around 2 hours with a USB-C cable. The case also supports wireless charging.

## Pairing and connection

Open the case lid and hold the button on the back for 3 seconds until the light flashes white. Then choose "Earbuds Pro" in your phone's Bluetooth list. The earbuds use Bluetooth 5.3 and can be connected to two devices at the same time, for example a phone and a laptop.

If the sound cuts out, move closer to your phone, make sure the earbuds are charged, and update the firmware in the companion app. Interference from Wi-Fi routers and microwave ovens can also cause drop-outs.

## One earbud is not working

If only one earbud plays sound, put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds and take them out again. If that does not help, reset them: with both earbuds in the case and the lid open, hold the back button for 10 seconds until the light flashes red three times. Then pair them again from the beginning.

## Sound and water resistance

Active noise cancelling can be switched between three modes: noise cancelling, transparency and off. Press and hold either earbud for 2 seconds to change the mode. The earbuds are rated IPX4, which means they survive sweat and light rain. They are not meant for swimming or showering.

## Warranty

The earbuds come with a 12-month warranty from the date of purchase. It covers manufacturing defects such as a dead battery or a faulty speaker. The warranty does not cover damage from drops, liquid beyond the IPX4 rating, or repairs made by anyone other than our service centres. Keep your invoice: it is your proof of purchase.

## Returns and refunds

You can return the earbuds within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are in working condition with all accessories. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 working days of us receiving the return. If you were charged twice, contact support with your order number and we will refund the duplicate payment.
