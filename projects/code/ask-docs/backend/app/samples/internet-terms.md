# HomeFiber broadband: service terms

## Plans and prices

HomeFiber offers three plans. The Basic plan gives 100 Mbps for ₹699 per month. The Plus plan gives 300 Mbps for ₹999 per month. The Max plan gives 1 Gbps for ₹1,799 per month. Prices include GST. A one-time installation fee of ₹1,500 applies to new connections.

## Fair usage

All plans include 3.3 TB of data each month. After that the speed is reduced to 5 Mbps until the next billing date. Streaming, video calls and large downloads all count towards the limit. We do not throttle any plan before the limit is reached.

## Billing

Your bill is issued on the first day of every month and is due within 15 days. If you pay later than that, a late fee of 2 percent of the bill is added for each month it stays unpaid. If the bill remains unpaid for 45 days, the connection may be suspended. You can pay by UPI, card or net banking, and we recommend switching on automatic payments.

## Outages and credits

If your connection is down for more than 24 hours because of a problem on our side, we credit one day of your monthly charge for every full day of the outage. Report outages through the app or by calling 1800-000-1234. Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance and does not qualify for credits.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time with 30 days of notice. If you cancel, we refund the unused part of your monthly charge, and the refund is processed in 10 to 15 working days. The installation fee is not refunded. You must return the router within 14 days of disconnection, or ₹2,000 will be charged for it.

## Moving house

If you move within our coverage area, we can shift your connection for ₹500. Ask at least 7 days before you move. The new address needs a site visit, which usually happens within 5 working days.

## Router and warranty

The router supplied with your plan is covered by a 24-month warranty against defects. Damage caused by power surges, water or opening the device is not covered. A faulty router is replaced free of charge on the next working day in most cities.
